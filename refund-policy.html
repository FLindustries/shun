The guarantee

30-day money-back guarantee: if a channel you blocked keeps showing up, or Shun isn't for you, email Shun support within 30 days of paying and get a full refund. No questions asked.

Shun's free plan has no payment, so there's nothing to refund there. The guarantee is for Shun Pro.

What it covers

  • Every Pro plan: Lifetime, Yearly and Monthly.
  • The full amount you paid for that payment, as long as you ask within 30 days of it.
  • Any reason, or none. If you tell us what went wrong, it helps fix it, but you don't have to.

Pro is sold by Shun's developer through ExtensionPay, with Stripe processing the payment. It isn't sold through Google or the Chrome Web Store, so refunds come from us, not from Google.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email Shun support (factuallogic98@gmail.com), ideally with “Shun refund” as the subject.
  2. Write from the email address you used at checkout, or tell us that address, so we can find the payment.
  3. If you have it, add the date of the payment. Never send card numbers: we don't need them.

What happens next

  • The developer refunds the payment in Stripe and replies to your email when it's done.
  • The money goes back to the card or payment method you paid with. Stripe says a refund usually takes 5 to 10 business days to show up, depending on your bank.
  • Your block lists and settings stay in your browser, and Shun's free features keep working.

Cancel a Monthly or Yearly plan

To cancel, email Shun support (factuallogic98@gmail.com) from the address you paid with and we cancel it for you, so you aren't charged again. Manage subscription in Shun's settings shows your plan. You can cancel without asking for a refund, and you can ask for both in the same email.
