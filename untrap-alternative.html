An UnTrap alternative with no subscription needed

UnTrap and RYS (now called Rein) both added paid plans that need an account. If what you really used them for was keeping certain channels and topics off YouTube, you may not need a subscription at all. Here's an honest comparison.

What changed at UnTrap

Short answerIn 2026 UnTrap introduced UnTrap Pro, a subscription, and moved some features that used to be free into it. On November 23, 2026, its checkout page showed $3.99 a month or $35.99 a year for a US address, with a 3-day free trial on the yearly plan. Pro is linked to an UnTrap account.

  • What stays free, according to its creator: core controls including hiding Shorts, home-page recommendations, related videos and comments, “along with other free functionality” (untrap.app/why-plus).
  • What Pro adds, according to its site: the full set of YouTube controls, presets, content filters (blocking topics, channels and keywords), video summaries, schedules and cross-browser sync. The site doesn't spell out how much filtering, if any, stays in the free plan.
  • Why: its creator wrote a page explaining that UnTrap is his full-time work, that App Store sales had funded the free browser versions, and that this revenue fell. It's a fair read if you want the other side (Why I introduced UnTrap Pro, October 2026).
  • Its Chrome listing shows about 100,000 users, 2.8 stars from 879 ratings, version 12.4, updated October 26, 2026, and “Offers in-app purchases”.

If Pro is worth it to you, it's a big, actively updated toolbox, and paying for it supports a developer who works on it full time. This page is for people who mainly used UnTrap for one job: keeping channels and topics out of their feed.

And RYS, now called Rein

Remove YouTube Suggestions is now listed as “Rein: Remove YouTube Suggestions”. On November 23, 2026, its Chrome listing showed about 10,000 users, 3.3 stars from 157 ratings, version 4.7.0, updated November 22, 2026. Free features include hiding homepage suggestions, Shorts, sidebar recommendations, end-of-video suggestions and comments. Signing in, with no payment, unlocks any 2 premium features; Premium unlocks the rest, at $3 a month or $24 a year according to its source code. It's open source. Rein hides parts of the page; it doesn't keep a channel or keyword blocklist.

Where UnTrap is stronger

A lot of places, and it's worth saying plainly. Shun doesn't have any of these:

  • Hundreds of options for hiding parts of YouTube: home-page recommendations, related videos, sidebars, live chat, notifications, buttons and more. Shun hides Shorts, comments and the “Most relevant” section in Subscriptions, and that's all.
  • Player tweaks, themes, layouts, keyboard shortcuts, autoplay control and home-page redirects.
  • Schedules and focus tools, AI video summaries, presets and cross-browser sync.
  • Versions for Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, including Safari on iPhone and iPad. Shun is Chrome on a computer only.

If what you want is mostly a calmer YouTube, with fewer things on the page, UnTrap's free plan may still cover you, and Unhook is free with no account. Shun won't replace either.

Where Shun is different, and where it's weaker

Different

  • Channel blocking is free and unlimited. “Block channel permanently” in YouTube's own 3-dot menu, or the Block this channel button next to Subscribe on video pages. The channel's videos are hidden on Home, search and Up next.
  • No account. Nothing to sign in to. Your lists stay in your browser; no analytics, no tracking.
  • No subscription needed. Pro is optional, and there's a one-time Lifetime option: $29.99 once (launch price until December 31, 2026). Or $19.99 a year, or $2.99 a month.
  • Keywords and 35 ready-made topics, matched as whole words in video titles. Free has 3 slots shared by keywords and topics.
  • A written promise: from v1.4.2 on, nothing free will move to Pro. (v1.4.2 itself made one change: adding your own words to a topic is Pro for new installs; earlier installs keep it free.)

Weaker

  • Chrome on a computer only. No Firefox, Edge listing, Safari, phones or TVs.
  • No hiding of recommendations, sidebars or other page elements beyond Shorts, comments and “Most relevant”.
  • No schedules, no sync, no summaries.
  • More than 3 keywords and topics needs Pro.
  • New, with few users, so it hasn't been tested by many people yet.
  • By design, blocks don't apply in your Subscriptions feed, and Shun doesn't change autoplay or end-screen suggestions.

Which one fits you

  • You use UnTrap for its many page tweaks, schedules or summaries: UnTrap Pro is the closest match. Shun doesn't do those things.
  • You want a calmer page, free, with no account: Unhook, or UnTrap's free plan.
  • You want to block specific channels, words or topics, without a subscription or an account: try Shun. Channel blocking is free, and Pro has a one-time option.
  • You want both: Unhook and Shun do different jobs. Unhook hides sections of the page; Shun blocks channels, words and topics.

Switching from UnTrap to Shun

  • Shun has no reader for UnTrap's settings files.
  • Channels: paste them into Shun, one per line (a name, an @handle or a channel link): in Shun's settings, open the Blocked tab and use “Import from another blocker”. Or block them as they show up, from the 3-dot menu or the Block this channel button. Step-by-step guide.
  • Muted topics: look for a matching ready-made topic in Shun's popup first; one switch covers a list of related words.
  • Shorts: one switch in Shun's popup. How hiding Shorts works.

Questions

How much does UnTrap Pro cost?

On November 23, 2026, UnTrap's Pro checkout page showed, for a US address, $3.99 a month or $35.99 a year, with a 3-day free trial on the yearly plan. Prices vary by country. Pro is linked to an UnTrap account.

Is Shun a full replacement for UnTrap?

No. UnTrap has hundreds of options for hiding parts of YouTube, plus schedules, video summaries and sync. Shun focuses on blocking channels, keywords and topics, and also hides Shorts and comments. If you mostly want to hide page elements, UnTrap's free plan or Unhook may suit you better.

Do I need an account or a subscription to use Shun?

No. There's no Shun account, and channel blocking is free and unlimited. Pro is optional: $2.99 a month, $19.99 a year, or $29.99 once for Lifetime (launch price until December 31, 2026). Checkout runs through ExtensionPay with Stripe, which asks for an email address for the purchase.

Can I import my UnTrap settings into Shun?

Not UnTrap's settings: Shun has no reader for UnTrap's files. You can paste your channels instead: in Shun's settings, open the Blocked tab, use “Import from another blocker” and paste channels one per line (a name, an @handle or a channel link). For words, switch on topics or add keywords in Shun's popup.
