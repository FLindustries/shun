How to turn off YouTube Shorts on desktop

YouTube doesn't have a switch that turns Shorts off. Here's what its own options do on a computer, and how to remove Shorts from Home, Subscriptions and search in Chrome.

Updated October 13, 2026. Written by the developer of Shun, the extension in the second half of this guide.

On this page

  1. YouTube's own options
  2. Hide Shorts with Shun
  3. What Hide Shorts removes

Short answerThere's no official setting to turn Shorts off on youtube.com. “Not interested” and “Show fewer Shorts” only reduce how many you see. To remove Shorts sections from Home, Subscriptions, search and the sidebar, use a browser extension. In Shun it's one switch: Hide Shorts.

YouTube's own options on a computer

There's no off switch

What you'll find
YouTube's settings have no option that turns Shorts off. How-To Geek reached the same conclusion in October 2025, and tbreak said the same about YouTube's mobile apps in April 2026.

“Not interested” on a Short

Where
The 3-dot menu on a Short in a Shorts shelf.
What it does
Feedback that one Short isn't for you, used for your recommendations.
Where it stops
One Short at a time. The shelf stays.

“Show fewer Shorts”

Where
YouTube documents it for the Home feed in the YouTube app: the 3-dot menu on a grid of Shorts. How-To Geek reports the same option on a computer.
What it does
Asks YouTube to show you fewer Shorts.
Where it stops
Fewer isn't none. The choice is saved in My Activity, and deleting it there undoes it.

The “X” that hides the shelf for 30 days

Where
Some guides describe an X on the Shorts shelf. In April 2026 tbreak described it on YouTube's mobile website (m.youtube.com).
What it does
Hides the Shorts shelf for 30 days.
Where it stops
It's a timer, not a setting, and it may not be there for you: How-To Geek wrote in October 2025 that the option no longer exists.

Turning off watch history

What it does
Since a 2023 change, YouTube leaves the Home feed mostly blank when watch history is off and you have little history saved (9to5Google, August 8, 2023). That removes Shorts from Home along with every other recommendation.
Where it stops
You lose all Home recommendations, and Shorts still appear in search and Subscriptions.

Hide Shorts with Shun

Shun is a free Chrome extension. Hide Shorts is part of the free plan, and it's off until you switch it on.

  1. Add Shun to Chrome

    Open Shun on the Chrome Web Store and click Add to Chrome. There's no account to create.

  2. Open the Shun popup

    Click the Shun icon in Chrome's toolbar. If it isn't there, open the puzzle-piece Extensions menu and pin Shun.

  3. Switch on Hide Shorts

    It's the first switch on the Controls tab. It applies right away in every open YouTube tab, without a reload.

  4. Optional: hide comments too

    Shun's Settings (the gear icon in the popup) have the same Hide Shorts switch, plus Hide YouTube comments, which removes the whole comments section under videos.

What Hide Shorts removes

  • Shorts shelves on Home
  • Shorts in your Subscriptions feed (Hide Shorts is one of the two Shun switches that work there)
  • Shorts in search results, and the Shorts chip in search filters
  • The Shorts entry in YouTube's sidebar

Shun applies it as the page opens, so Shorts sections don't flash up and then vanish.

What it doesn't do: a Short opened from a direct link still plays, and Shun can't change the YouTube app on phones, tablets or TVs. YouTube also changes its pages often; if Shorts start showing up somewhere new, use Report a Bug in Shun's Settings or email Shun support (factuallogic98@gmail.com).

Hide only some Shorts

If you like Shorts but not certain creators or subjects, leave Hide Shorts off and block those instead:

  • Block the channel. In the Shorts player, a blocked channel's Short is hidden and paused, so you don't hear it, and playback moves on to the next one that isn't blocked. In search results, a blocked channel's Shorts are hidden inside Shorts shelves too: a Short there doesn't show its channel, so Shun asks youtube.com who made it (only the Short's ID is sent, and only while a channel is blocked). How to block a channel.
  • Block a topic or a word. The same happens in the Shorts player for a topic you've blocked, such as Gaming, Pranks or Brainrot, and Shorts with that topic or word in the title leave Shorts shelves too.
