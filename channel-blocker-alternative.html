A Channel Blocker alternative for Chrome

Channel Blocker is still on the Chrome Web Store, it's free, and plenty of people use it. If it's missing channels for you, or you'd like something that works from YouTube's own menus, here's an honest look at the options, Shun included.

Is Channel Blocker still working in 2026?

Short answerIt's still available. As of November 23, 2026, Channel Blocker's Chrome Web Store page offers “Add to Chrome” and shows version 3.0.5, updated August 5, 2024, about 100,000 users and 2.7 stars from 826 ratings. It carries the store's “Featured” and “Follows recommended practices” badges.

What the public record shows, as of November 23, 2026:

  • The listing says it was rebuilt from scratch for Manifest V3 and is open source. Its GitHub repository isn't archived; its latest commit is from August 4, 2024.
  • The repository has 29 open issues and pull requests. Recent open issues include channels not being blocked on YouTube's multi-channel uploads (#60, January 2026) and blocked channels not disappearing until the page is reloaded (#65, April 2026).
  • The Firefox version, on Firefox Add-ons, is version 2.2.4, last updated August 6, 2022, with about 7,400 daily users.

None of that means it has stopped working for everyone. If it does what you need, there's no reason to switch.

Where Channel Blocker is stronger

  • Comments. It blocks comments by user, and its listing says it can block comments by regular expression too. Shun can only hide all comments.
  • Regular expressions for fine-grained rules. Shun only matches whole words.
  • Entirely free, supported by Patreon and donations, and open source.
  • A Firefox version. Shun is Chrome only.
  • Many more users, so it has been tried by far more people than Shun has.

Where Shun is different, and where it's weaker

Different

  • Blocking inside YouTube's own 3-dot menu: “Block channel permanently” (when you're signed in, right after “Don't recommend channel”). Plus a “Block this channel” button next to Subscribe on video pages. (Channel Blocker also offers one-click blocking, with its own buttons.)
  • Hidden where you look: Home, search, Up next, channel pages, playlists and the Shorts player. Matched by name and, in search results and playlists, by @handle.
  • Whole words, no patterns. “game” hides “Game night” but not “Endgame”.
  • 35 ready-made topics. One switch blocks a subject with its own list of related terms.
  • One switch each for Shorts, comments, and the “Most relevant” section in Subscriptions.
  • Show just now lets you see a blocked channel once without unblocking it.

Weaker

  • Chrome on a computer only. No Firefox, no phones or TVs.
  • No regular expressions and no comment blocking by user.
  • Not entirely free: channel blocking is unlimited, but keywords and topics share 3 free slots. More needs Pro.
  • New, with few users, so it hasn't been tested by many people yet.
  • By design, blocks don't apply in your Subscriptions feed, and Shun doesn't change autoplay or end-screen suggestions.
  • Like every YouTube filter, it can miss things when YouTube changes its pages, until an update ships.

Which one fits you

  • Channel Blocker works for you: keep it.
  • You want regex, or to block commenters: BlockTube or YTBlock, both still updated in 2026 per their listings.
  • You use Firefox: Channel Blocker or BlockTube for Firefox.
  • You want to block a channel from YouTube's own menu and have it stay out of Home, search and Up next, without writing patterns: try Shun.

Switching from Channel Blocker to Shun

  • Shun can't import Channel Blocker's lists. Its Import only reads files exported from Shun.
  • Channels: block them again as they show up, from the 3-dot menu or the Block this channel button. Unlimited on the free plan. Step-by-step guide.
  • Regex rules: type the plain words and phrases into Custom Keywords instead. Free has 3 slots shared by keywords and topics.
  • Try a topic first: one topic switch often covers a list of words you'd otherwise type out.
  • You can keep Channel Blocker installed while you try Shun. Each keeps its own list.

Questions

Is Channel Blocker still on the Chrome Web Store?

Yes. As of November 23, 2026, its Chrome Web Store listing offers Add to Chrome and shows version 3.0.5, last updated August 5, 2024, about 100,000 users and a 2.7-star rating from 826 ratings.

Can Shun import my Channel Blocker list?

No. Shun's Import only reads files exported from Shun. You block channels again as they show up, from a video's 3-dot menu or the Block this channel button. Channel blocking is unlimited on Shun's free plan.

Does Shun support regular expressions like Channel Blocker?

No. Shun matches whole words in video titles instead: blocking “game” hides “Game night” but not “Endgame”. If you rely on regular expressions or on blocking comments by user, Channel Blocker, BlockTube or YTBlock suit you better.

Is Shun free?

Channel blocking is free and unlimited, with no account. Keywords and topics share 3 free slots. Pro adds unlimited keywords and topics, your own words inside topics, Strict Mode, a log of hidden videos and exceptions for subscribed channels, for $2.99 a month, $19.99 a year or $29.99 once (launch price until December 31, 2026).
